Keys and saves

Type normally. Ctrl+letter sends control characters. Alt is Open Apple (button 0), right Alt is Closed Apple (button 1). Ctrl+F12 is Ctrl-Reset.

With “Joystick keys” on, the arrow keys move the stick and Space/Z and X are the two buttons. A connected gamepad works as well.

Changes written to a disk are kept in this browser and survive reloads. “Download disk” exports an image; “Discard saves” restores the original disks.
