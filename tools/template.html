THE SHARD OF SPRING SSI, 1986 · runs in your browser

Spellbook 法術書 — 遊戲問 "Cast what spell?" 時，點法術名就會自動打字輸入

SP 欄 = 該法術至少要投入的法術點數，投入到愈多效果愈強。使用場合：營地 = Camp 選單按 C 選角色；戰鬥 = 戰鬥回合按 C(需 ≥3 移動點)；施法後的目標、SP 數量照遊戲提示回答。你的角色必須「會該流派」的法術，否則遊戲會說 "You don't know that spell!"。

How to play

Press any key at the title screen, then choose L to load a party. A ready-made party is stored in slot 5 (Redwind, Erleath, Cusiro and Holm). Use C at the menu to create your own characters and parties.

On the map: Return moves forward, ← and → turn, C opens the camp menu, P shows party information and S saves. Everything else is menu driven; Esc backs out of most screens.

To cast a spell: pick C in the camp menu (or C during your combat turn), choose a character, then type the spell's name exactly — or click its name in the Spellbook below. The spell list comes straight from the game's own spell file.

Progress is saved in this browser. The original asked you to swap disks; here the disk changes happen automatically.
